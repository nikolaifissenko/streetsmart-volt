# Reel cover prompts: Rome bike POV (rotation bank) — v2

10 Gemini prompts for 9:16 reel covers. Each one stands alone, so just copy and paste it.
v2 (17 Nov 2026) after the first test round. Nikolai's notes:
- Daytime only, sunset at the latest. No night shots.
- More traffic, more chaos, more anxiety.
- Real streets, with Italian traffic that looks right.
- No Appia Antica, and no Fori Imperiali (closed to cars).

The ones he liked in round 1 (Piazza Venezia in the rain, Via del Corso in a shower,
Monti at sunset, Trastevere in autumn) are kept here with more traffic added.

**Built into every prompt:** Rome's taxis are WHITE (Gemini defaults to yellow), traffic drives on the
right, small Italian cars, scooters weaving in, threats from close range (a bus at the elbow, a car door
opening, a scooter cutting in). Hands look right, no recognizable faces, plates unreadable, no text, top third simple.

**If the street still looks made up:** attach a real photo of that street (one of yours from the
tours) and add *"Use the attached photo as reference for the exact street layout and buildings."*
That's the only reliable fix, because Gemini doesn't know Rome's geography street by street.
