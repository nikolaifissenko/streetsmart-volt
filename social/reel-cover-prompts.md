# Reel cover prompts: Rome bike POV (rotation bank)

10 ready-to-use Gemini prompts for 9:16 reel covers. Each one stands alone, so just copy and paste it.
Rotate through them in order and note the date next to each when you use it, so the grid doesn't repeat.

**Rules built into every prompt:** vertical 9:16, first-person POV over city-bike handlebars,
anatomically correct hands, no recognizable faces, unreadable plates, no text or logos,
**top third kept simple** so there's room for the reel's title text. Keep that third clean
even when the scene is busy: it's where the hook goes.

**If it comes out wrong:** deformed hands → regenerate. Readable text or plates → add
"no readable text anywhere". Too "postcard" or too clean → add "candid, slightly imperfect
smartphone photo, not a stock image".
